Design Engineering // Blueprint Grid

Construction Geometry & Grid System

Precision technical specifications defining stroke radiuses, coordinate anchors, and mathematical proportions.

01 // OUTER SQUIRCLE

90x90 unit rounded square with a 22px corner radius, balancing modern software interface squircle geometry with structural containment.

02 // CYRIL ARC (C)

8px stroke width arc spanning from (65, 30) through apex (25, 50) to (65, 70), establishing the initial C for Cyril and Computing.

03 // JHUNE ANCHOR (J)

Intertwined vertical vector plunging from (52, 28) to (52, 61), smoothly curving left into a 15px radius baseline hook for Jhune.

04 // COMPUTING BIT (NODE)

Concentric focus circle at coordinate (68, 50) representing binary data, technical precision, and the Canabal initial.

Brand Philosophy

Meaning of Shapes and Colors

The CJC Cyber Monogram interlocks the initials Cyril, Jhune, and Canabal into a unified technical emblem, symbolizing software engineering structure, personal discipline, and modern computing ambition.

SHAPE ARCHITECTURE

The outer squircle signifies stability and modular architecture. The interlocking C and J strokes symbolize continuous integration, while the right computing node anchors the composition like a focused terminal cursor.

CHROMATIC HARMONY

Electric cobalt #5b7cff expresses intellectual energy and digital precision. It is anchored by obsidian #14151c for depth and pure white #ffffff for immediate typographic clarity across dark and light viewports.
